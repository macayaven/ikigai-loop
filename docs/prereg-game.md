# Catch the AI: the study plan, registered before the first answer

Registered by the commit that adds this file, merged before the study collected any answer.

## Question

When a short sentence an AI wrote about a small card hides a trap, how often do people catch it, compared with a checker that compares numbers, names, negations and quantity words with the card?

## Participants

About 100 adults on Prolific (UK residents, fluent in English, on a desktop or laptop), paid £0.60 for about four minutes regardless of accuracy, through the study version of the page (`?src=prolific`). A pilot of five comes first and is excluded. Ordinary visitors send nothing and are not part of this study.

## Procedure

Consent, then the instructions, then "Have you played Catch the AI before?", then a one-question instruction check, then the six rounds in one of six cyclic rotations picked at random. No feedback until all six answers are in. The participant submits a pre-filled Google Form, whose confirmation page holds the completion link. Then a debrief and the full reveal.

## Primary outcome

The share of valid games that answer "It doesn't" on round 6 (the card says 87 unread messages; the sentence says 86). The checker flags round 6 every time.

## Secondary outcomes

- The share answering correctly on each of the six rounds.
- Round 4 (the card is for this season; the sentence says last season): the share answering "It doesn't". The checker passes round 4 by design.
- The share catching at least one of the two number traps, rounds 2 and 6.
- The spread of correct answers out of six, next to the checker's 5 of 6.

Rounds 4 and 5 are called "not supported by the card", not false.

## Sample and stopping

Target: 100 valid games. Collection stops at Nov 4, 18:00 Madrid, or when Prolific's places are filled, whichever comes first. With fewer than 30 valid games, only raw counts are reported.

## Exclusions, fixed now

- Pilot participants.
- Anyone who answered "Yes" to having played before.
- Duplicate hashed Prolific IDs: only the first submission counts.
- Missing or unparseable payloads.

Sensitivity analysis only, reported but not excluded: games with any answer faster than 1 second; games that chose Skip on the instruction check; games that answered "It doesn't" on both rounds 1 and 3, the two supported sentences.

## Analysis

Counts with exact (Clopper-Pearson) 95% intervals for every outcome. No significance tests. All six rounds are reported, whatever they show. The rotation is reported as a check. Every completed submission is paid, including excluded ones.

## What this can't show

Six made-up examples are not a measure of general human or AI accuracy. The sample is UK desktop users on a paid panel.

## Deviations

Not run before the submission deadline (decided Nov 3, 19:55 Madrid): six independent reviewers judged its expected value too small for the time left. The study version stays on the page, closed until a form is set. No answers were collected and no results exist.
