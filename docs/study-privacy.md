# Privacy notice: the Catch the AI study

- **Who:** Carlos Crespo Macaya, a solo hackathon developer, is responsible for this data. Contact: Prolific's message system for this study.
- **What:** your six answers, the time each took, a screen-size category (small, medium or large), whether you had played before, your answer to one instruction check, the order you saw the rounds in, the study ID, and a scrambled form (a one-way hash) of your Prolific participant ID. Not your raw ID, no name, no email, nothing you type.
- **Why:** to report, in aggregate, how often people catch planted mistakes, in a public hackathon write-up.
- **Legal basis:** your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw it at any time through Prolific, without losing payment you have earned.
- **Where it goes:** the answers are stored in a Google Form and its sheet, which only I can open, under Google's terms, which may include processing outside the EU. Prolific handles recruitment and payment under its own terms. Google and Prolific may process network data such as IP addresses; I don't see or keep it.
- **How long:** raw study records are deleted by December 4, 2026. Published results are aggregate only.
- **Your rights:** access, correction, deletion and withdrawal, through Prolific's message system; send your Prolific ID there and I can find your row from it. You can also complain to the Spanish data protection authority (AEPD, aepd.es).
