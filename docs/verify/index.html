Verify claims in your agent's output

Give it your agent's claims and your own records as JSON. See which numbers and sources don't reconcile.

This example is made up: the claim says 9 and the records say 5. Replace that JSON with your own, then press Run.

This check covers parseable numbers, cited sources, labels and direction. It does not check that a sentence is true. Without labels it cannot tell which metric a number belongs to, and a cited fact is only checked to exist.

Everything runs on your machine. I load the checker once, then nothing is sent anywhere.

The input holds your agent's claims plus your own records: facts and metrics come from your system of record, never from the agent's output. Every field is in the worked example, and the adapter recipe maps another shape to it.
