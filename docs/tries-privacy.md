# Privacy notice: saving your checker tries

- **Who:** Carlos Crespo Macaya, a solo hackathon developer, is responsible for this data. Contact: the project's GitHub repository.
- **What:** each card and sentence you check, and what the checker said, saved without your name, email or IP address. A random visitor code groups one browser's tries. No IP address, user agent, cookies, referrer, location, screen size or seconds of the clock are saved.
- **Why:** to find where the checker gets things wrong, and to quote anonymous examples when writing about the project.
- **Legal basis:** your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw it at any time using "Stop saving" or "Delete my tries".
- **Where it goes:** the tries are stored in a Cloudflare D1 database behind a Cloudflare Worker, under Cloudflare's terms.
- **How long:** rows are deleted 12 months after they are saved, or at once on your request.
- **Your rights:** access, correction, deletion and withdrawal. Use "Delete my tries" on the checker page, or ask Carlos with your visitor code. You can also complain to the Spanish data protection authority (AEPD, aepd.es).
