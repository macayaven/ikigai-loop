compare models, and the product prompt passed the gate on 10 of 10 first
drafts on Nov 1, so a live run might show the gate catching nothing. After
this ticket, anyone with a key or a subscription can reproduce two answers
from committed artefacts alone: how the five pinned rows compare on the same
fixed synthetic case set through the same gate, and what the gate stops when
the prompt does not teach its rules.
