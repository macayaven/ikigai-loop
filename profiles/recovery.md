## Limits (the app must respect these)

Profile A's limits apply (private categories, quiet hours, never-read sources, the repo guard). Profile B adds:

- **Health: daily aggregates only.** Never diagnoses, medical details or lab results.
- **No pushing towards programming** in this profile.
- **Never reads the content of private sessions** (see the private list).
- **Journal:** the app may read the full text and nothing is off-limits, but nothing from it is ever made public without my explicit consent.
- **No bike proposals before Dec 1**, when the bike exists.
- **After 21:00:** decompress. Something funny, music, getting ready for bed; lights out by 00:00. The app sends at most one wind-down nudge, at 23:00, and never proposes tasks.
