**Who:** Lucía, 38, ICU nurse on rotating shifts, two kids (6 and 9), lives in Valencia.
**Version:** 1 · 2026-10-27

---

## 1. What I love
*Weight: 0.35*

- I lose track of time throwing pots on the wheel. I want at least 2 sessions a week, 90 min each.
- Swimming in the sea before 9:00 from May to October. In winter, the indoor pool.
- Cooking slow food on Sundays with the kids helping, even if it takes twice as long.

## 2. What I'm good at
*Weight: 0.20*

- Staying calm when things go wrong at 3 a.m. and explaining it simply to scared families.
- Teaching new nurses: I've mentored 6 in two years and they still message me.
- Hand skills: glazing, small repairs, sewing.

## 3. What the world needs (from me)
*Weight: 0.25*

- My kids need me present on my days off, not on my phone.
- Junior nurses in my unit need practical guides that don't exist yet. I'd like to write 1 a month.
- My mother (78) needs a call every other day.

## 4. What I can be paid for
*Weight: 0.20*

- My nursing salary, which depends on not burning out: max 4 night shifts in a row.
- Selling ceramics at the Saturday market, 2 times a month, aiming for €150 per market.

---

## Limits (the app must respect these)

- Never schedule anything in the 10 hours after a night shift. That's sleep.
- No notifications between 22:00 and 7:30, or during a shift.
- Don't show me health or weight metrics. I see enough of that at work.
- Kids' names and photos stay out of the app.

## Signals I'll share

| Source | What it tells the app | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Google Calendar (shifts calendar only) | When I'm free, after nights | Read-only |
| Spotify | What I listen to at the wheel | Recently played only |
| A daily 1-tap check-in | Energy 1–5 | I answer it in the app |

## What "better" would look like in 2 weeks

- I did 4+ ceramic sessions and had glazed pieces ready for the market.
- I called my mother at least 6 times without needing to remember it myself.
- I finished the first guide for junior nurses (just a draft is fine).

---

### Why it's written like this

From these lines Wonder drafts **yes/no criteria** that the judges check on every new version of the app. For example:

- *"The home screen shows the next free 90-minute slot for ceramics."* (love)
- *"No block proposes an activity within 10 h after a night shift."* (limit)
- *"A reminder to call Mum appears on alternate days, never during a shift."* (world needs)
- *"No block shows weight or health metrics."* (limit)

You approve or edit those criteria before anything runs. Vague lines like "I love creativity" can't become a check, so the app can't optimize for them. Numbers, times and "never" rules can.
